The Problem

A tragedy disguised
as tradition.

23 million girls in India drop out of school every month because of menstruation. Not because they want to quit. Simply because their bodies do what they're supposed to — and no one prepared them for it.

She lacks clean pads. The washroom has no door. Nobody told her this was normal. She stays home. She falls behind. She gives up. This is not okay.

This isn't a hygiene problem. It's an education crisis.
