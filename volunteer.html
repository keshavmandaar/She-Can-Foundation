Before You Apply

Common questions.

What is the time commitment? +

It depends on the role. Mentors usually commit 2 hours a week. On-ground drives happen on weekends, typically 3–4 hours. We respect your schedule.

Where are your chapters located? +

We have active chapters in Delhi, Jaipur, Lucknow, and Kochi. Digital volunteers can contribute from anywhere in India or abroad.

Do I need teaching experience? +

Not at all. We provide training and session outlines. Your patience and consistency matter far more than formal qualifications.

Do you partner with corporates for CSR? +

Yes, we run employee engagement drives and CSR partnerships. Email partnerships@shecanfoundation.org to get started.

Is volunteering safe for women? +

Absolutely. All field visits are conducted in groups with senior volunteers present. We never send anyone alone and all locations are vetted.
