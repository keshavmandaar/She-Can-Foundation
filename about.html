Our Story

Global Vision.
Local Action.

She Can Foundation was born from a stubborn belief — that every woman deserves a chance to write her own story. Not just the ones born into privilege.

Since 2015, we've been on the ground: in villages, in shelters, on streets. We work with women who've been told "no" their entire lives. Our job is to replace that "no" with resources, community, and possibility.

Our Journey

Nine years on the ground.

2015

She Can Foundation Founded

Started by young women in Delhi with one mission: reach those no one else reaches.

2016

First Period Power Drive

Distributed 500 sanitary kits across three government schools. The response changed everything.

2017

EduShe Street Classrooms Launch

Yamuna Pushta. Children who had never held a pencil started writing their names.

2019

Expanded to 15 States

Community circles spread — Jaipur, Lucknow, Kochi, Kolkata. Thousands joined the movement.

2021

COVID-19 Response

Served 3,000+ meals and distributed hygiene kits. A crisis doesn't stop the work.

2024

12,000+ Lives Touched

Nine years in. Still showing up. Still believing every woman can.
